This project adapts selected algorithm ideas from face-mesh for its existing MediaPipe Face Landmarker output: 1-Euro smoothing of pose angles and developer-preview landmark coordinates, EAR-based blink event counting, and temporary centroid-based association between adjacent frames. The upstream detector, application structure, and unrelated features are not included. Adaptations use this repository's feature schema and may differ from upstream implementation.
